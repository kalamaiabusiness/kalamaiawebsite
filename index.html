A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

How is my customer ID generated?

The ID combines your first name and the last four digits of your WhatsApp number, for example Agung6789. If that ID already exists, the system adds a short suffix to keep IDs unique.

How do I track an order?

After placing an order, save the order reference shown on screen. Use that reference together with your customer ID to see the latest status and shipment tracking details.

Where are customer and order details stored?

After the Google Apps Script backend is configured and deployed, customer profiles and orders are saved to separate tabs in Google Sheets. The team can download the sheet as Excel or CSV.
